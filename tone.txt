don't take shit too seriously
life is fleeting and everyone ends up dead
and in a time of despair
dark humor and comedic art
may be a saving grace
a super villain taking over the world
would be ironic
if they had one hand in their pocket

decision making must be chill
to channel the motivation of love

action must be swift and decisive
when stepping into the ring
rage and aggression
is a necessary tool for the warrior

not everything makes sentence sense

strive for artistic perfection
but acknowledge the prevalence and power of shit
if there is a seed of truth in it
it might not survive without it

the crude ore of chaos continues
but hopefully it can be refined
